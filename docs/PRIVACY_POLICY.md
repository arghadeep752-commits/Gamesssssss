# Hime Privacy Policy

Hime is built so that we can deliver your messages and calls without being able to read or listen to them. This policy explains what Hime collects, why, and the choices you have.

## What we cannot see

Messages, photos, videos, voice messages, documents, contacts and locations you send are end-to-end encrypted on your device. Each of your devices has its own private key that never leaves it. Our servers store only encrypted data and cannot decrypt it. Voice and video calls are peer-to-peer and encrypted with DTLS-SRTP; when a relay server is needed it only forwards encrypted packets.

## What we collect

Account information: your email address and/or phone number (used to sign in), your display name, optional username, about text and profile photo.

Device information: a device name, platform, app version and a public encryption key for each signed-in device, so others can encrypt messages for it. If you allow notifications, a push token.

Delivery information: who is in which conversation, when encrypted messages were sent, and delivery/read watermarks (unless you turn read receipts off), so messages can be delivered in order and synced to your devices.

Call records: who called whom, when, and for how long, shown in your call history.

Status updates: status text, photos and videos are protected by access controls so only the audience you choose can see them, and are deleted after 24 hours. They are not end-to-end encrypted.

Contact discovery (optional): if you choose to find friends from your phone contacts, phone numbers are converted to SHA-256 hashes on your device and compared with the hashes of people who chose to be discoverable by phone. We do not store your address book.

Reports: if you report someone, the report and any messages you choose to include are shared with our safety team.

## What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not show ads. We do not read your messages. We do not use your content to train AI models.

## Retention

Encrypted message data stays on our servers until it is deleted for everyone, or the conversation's members leave, or the account is deleted. Expired status updates are removed automatically. Media of deleted messages is removed from storage.

## Your choices

You control who sees your last seen, online status, profile photo, about and status updates, who can add you to groups and who can call you. You can block people, turn off read receipts, export your data and permanently delete your account from Settings → Account.

## Security

Data in transit is protected with TLS. Access to stored data is enforced by row-level security policies. Local copies of your messages are encrypted on your phone with a key kept in the Android Keystore.

## Children

Hime is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country).

## Changes and contact

We will notify you in the app before material changes to this policy take effect. Questions can be sent to the operator of the Hime server you use.
