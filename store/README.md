# MOULIKA - Play Store screenshots

Eight landscape screenshots (1920x1080 JPEG), rendered from the game and captioned in its chalk font, numbered in upload order.
Pushing to this branch republishes them on the `moulika-store` release.

The app icon: `moulika_icon_512.png` for Google Play (512x512 PNG, full square; the store rounds the corners), `moulika_icon_1024.png` a bigger copy.
