# The Epic Tales of Mahabharata – Part 1 (concept trailer)

Production files for the Suprio Studio concept trailer.

- `assets/` – key art (Krishna & Arjuna silhouettes, title logo) and the parallax layers cut from it
- `blender/scenes.py` – Blender 4.2 scenes built from the Hyper3D Rodin models (armies, crown, wheel, Krishna, Arjuna)
- `render/` – compositor, titles, VFX and sound mix used to cut the trailer
- `fonts/` – open-licence fonts (Cardo, TeX Gyre Pagella, FreeSerif, Poppins)

Stock footage and music are licensed Adobe Stock assets and are not stored in this repository.
